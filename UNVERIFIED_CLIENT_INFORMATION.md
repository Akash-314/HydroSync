# HydroSync Innovations LLP — Unverified Client Information Checklist

The following items represent **genuine corporate information** required from the designated partners / authorized leadership of HydroSync Innovations LLP. 

Once provided, these items can be directly configured in `src/config/site.ts` or their respective data files to enrich the live website.

---

## 1. Statutory & Corporate Registration

- [ ] **LLP Identification Number (LLPIN):** Official Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) registration number.
- [ ] **Incorporation Date:** Official date of incorporation as stated in the Certificate of Incorporation.
- [ ] **Registered Office Address:** Full legal registered postal address including door/building number, street name, and PIN code.
- [ ] **GSTIN (Optional / If applicable):** Goods and Services Tax Identification Number for invoicing clarity.
- [ ] **MSME / Startup India Registration (Optional / If applicable):** Udyam or DPIIT recognition number if registered.

---

## 2. Official Corporate Communication Channels

- [ ] **Official Telephone / Landline / Mobile Number:** Dedicated business contact number for public enquiries.
- [ ] **Official WhatsApp Business Number:** Direct WhatsApp channel for student and institutional queries.
- [ ] **Corporate Postal / Facility Address:** Physical campus, laboratory, or office facility address if different from the registered office.

---

## 3. Leadership & Key Team Members

- [ ] **Designated Partners / Founders:**
  - Full legal name
  - Official corporate designation (e.g., Designated Partner, Managing Director, Technical Director)
  - Professional biography (50–100 words outlining verified technical domain experience)
  - High-resolution professional headshot photograph
  - Verified LinkedIn profile URL
- [ ] **Key Engineering Division Leads (Optional):**
  - Department leads (IoT, Embedded Hardware, Academic Programs)

---

## 4. Academic & Institutional Engagements

- [ ] **Verified Partner Colleges & Universities:**
  - Official institution names where MoUs, workshops, or training programs have been formally executed.
  - Written consent / brand usage guidelines to display college logos on `/for-colleges` and `/partners`.
- [ ] **Institutional Testimonials / Endorsements:**
  - Signed quotes or email endorsements from faculty coordinators, HODs, or principals with authorization to display their name, designation, and institution.

---

## 5. Commercial Project Case Studies & R&D Milestones

- [ ] **Publicly Disclosable Projects:**
  - Client name or industry domain (if under NDA, generalized as "Tier-1 AgriTech OEM", etc.)
  - Technical architecture summary (microcontroller, sensors, communication protocols)
  - Genuine field performance results or problem-solution narrative.
  - Photographs of actual prototype hardware, PCB layouts, or deployed installations.

---

## 6. Official Media & Accreditations

- [ ] **Press Releases / Media Coverage:**
  - Direct links to published articles in reputable newspapers, tech portals, or university journals.
- [ ] **Awards & Recognition:**
  - Legitimate competition wins, hackathon mentorship awards, or government grants with verification certificates.
